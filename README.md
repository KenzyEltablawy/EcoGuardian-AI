## 📖 Overview

EcoGuardian AI is a lightweight decision support system that combines environmental indicators with machine learning to recommend climate interventions for cities worldwide.

The system integrates:
- **Temperature**, **Humidity**, **Wind Speed**
- **PM2.5** and **PM10** particulate matter
- Engineered risk indicators: **Heat Risk**, **Pollution Risk**, **Climate Severity**

A **Random Forest classifier** categorizes environmental conditions into six intervention strategies:
- 🌿 Green Roof
- ☀️ Cool Roof
- 🌳 Plant Trees
- 🏭 Air Pollution Control
- 🔀 Mixed Strategy
- ✅ No Immediate Action

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Urban areas face escalating environmental challenges:
- **Urban Heat Islands** — cities are warmer than surrounding rural areas
- **Particulate Air Pollution** — PM2.5 and PM10 pose serious health risks

Existing tools often focus on single variables and require specialized expertise. EcoGuardian AI bridges this gap by providing an accessible, multi-indicator decision support tool.
